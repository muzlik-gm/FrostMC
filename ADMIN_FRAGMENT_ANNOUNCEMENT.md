# 🔥 FROSTSMP FRAGMENT SYSTEM - LAUNCH

## 🌟 10 ELEMENTAL FRAGMENTS
Master **Fire, Water, Air, Earth, Dark, Light, Void, Storm, Dragon, and Mob** - each with **4-5 unique abilities** (40+ total). Fire unleashes pyromancy with Fireball and Phoenix Rebirth, Water heals with Healing Wave and Tsunami, Air grants flight and mobility, Earth provides defense with Stone Skin, Dark drains life, Light buffs allies, Void manipulates reality, Storm controls lightning, Dragon brings ultimate destruction, and Mob summons creatures to fight for you.

## 🔮 RITUAL SYSTEM
Build **multi-block structures** and place special items to perform rituals. The **Fragment Creation Ritual** (10-15 minutes) unlocks a new fragment, while the **Fragment Changer Ritual** (5 minutes) lets you switch between owned fragments. Stay within range during the ritual or it fails!

## ⚡ DUAL PROGRESSION SYSTEM
**Levels (1-50):** Gain XP by using abilities and defeating enemies. Each level gives **cooldown reduction, damage boost, and cost reduction** (up to 30% at max level). **Ranks (1-8):** Unlock new abilities and passive powers as you rank up. Higher ranks unlock ultimate abilities - Fire Rank 5 grants Phoenix Rebirth (revive on death), Dragon Rank 8 unlocks Dragon Ascension (become a dragon), and more!

## 🎮 GAMEPLAY FEATURES
Use **Sneak + Click** to cast abilities from hotbar slots 0-4. Track your progress with the **custom GUI** (`/fragment gui`) showing all fragments, abilities, and stats. Experience **epic particle effects** that scale with your rank - higher ranks = more impressive visuals. Defeat **bosses for bonus XP** to level faster. Switch between fragments strategically for different situations!

## 🚀 GET STARTED
Type `/fragment gui` to open the fragment menu and see your collection. Complete your first ritual to unlock a fragment and begin your journey to elemental mastery!
