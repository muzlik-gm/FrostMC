## Overview

The FrostSMP plugin features **13 custom crafting recipes** divided into three categories:
- **10 Fragment Creation Recipes** - Create ritual items to obtain Fragments
- **1 Fragment Changer Recipe** - Switch between owned Fragments
- **1 Ritual Catalyst Recipe** - Component for rituals (currently unused)
- **1 Mana Flask Recipe** - Consumable mana restoration item

All recipes use **shaped crafting** and require a **3x3 crafting table**.

---

## How Crafting Works

### Recipe Pattern System

All Fragment Creation recipes follow the same pattern:
```
[O] [I] [O]
[I] [C] [I]
[O] [I] [O]

O = Outer Material (4x required)
I = Inner Material (4x required)
C = Center Material (1x Diamond - always)
```

### Fragment Creation Process

1. **Craft the Item** → Receive a Nether Star with Fragment name
2. **Place the Item** → Drop/place the Nether Star on the ground
3. **Start Ritual** → Ritual begins automatically
4. **Wait 10-15 minutes** → Stay within 5 blocks of the item
5. **Receive Fragment** → Fragment is granted automatically upon completion

## Fragment Creation Recipes

### Starter Fragments (Rank 2-3)

#### 🔥 Fire Fragment (Base Rank 3)
**Theme:** Aggressive damage-over-time, ignition, and explosive abilities

**Recipe Pattern:**
```
[Netherrack] [Fire Charge] [Netherrack]
[Fire Charge]   [Diamond]   [Fire Charge]
[Netherrack] [Fire Charge] [Netherrack]
```

**Materials Required:**
- 4x Netherrack
- 4x Fire Charge
- 1x Diamond

**Material Sources:**
- **Netherrack:** Nether dimension (abundant)
- **Fire Charge:** Craft with Blaze Powder + Coal + Gunpowder
- **Diamond:** Mine at Y-level -59 to -64

**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐ Easy (requires Nether access)

---

**Recipe Pattern:**

```
[Prismarine] [Water Bucket] [Prismarine]
[Water Bucket]  [Diamond]   [Water Bucket]
[Prismarine] [Water Bucket] [Prismarine]
```

### Intermediate Fragments (Rank 4-5)

#### 🌑 Dark Fragment (Base Rank 4)
**Theme:** Debuffs, life-steal, shadow mechanics, and vampiric abilities

**Recipe Pattern:**
```
  [Obsidian]   [Wither Skeleton Skull]   [Obsidian]
[Wither Skeleton Skull]    [Diamond]    [Wither Skeleton Skull]
  [Obsidian]   [Wither Skeleton Skull]   [Obsidian]
```

**Materials Required:**
- 4x Obsidian
- 4x Wither Skeleton Skull
- 1x Diamond

**Material Sources:**
- **Obsidian:** Mine with diamond pickaxe (lava + water)
- **Wither Skeleton Skull:** Kill wither skeletons in Nether fortresses
  - Drop rate: 2.5% base, 5.5% with Looting III
- **Diamond:** Mine at Y-level -59 to -64

**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Hard (rare skull drops)

**Farming Tips:**
- Use Looting III sword
- Build a farm in Nether fortress
- Expect to kill 20-40 wither skeletons per skull

**Theme:** Buffs, purification, radiant damage, and holy abilities

**Recipe Pattern:**
```
[Glowstone]  [Beacon]  [Glowstone]
  [Beacon]  [Diamond]   [Beacon]
[Glowstone]  [Beacon]  [Glowstone]
```

**Materials Required:**
- 4x Glowstone
- 4x Beacon
- 1x Diamond

**Material Sources:**
- **Glowstone:** Nether dimension (ceiling and walls)
- **Beacon:** Craft with 5 glass + 3 obsidian + 1 nether star

**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Hard (requires Wither boss)

**Boss Requirements:**
- Need 3 wither skeleton skulls + 4 soul sand per Wither
- Need 4 Wither kills for 4 beacons

### Advanced Fragments (Rank 6-8)

#### ⚡ Storm Fragment (Base Rank 6)
**Theme:** Lightning, weather control, chain effects, and electricity

**Recipe Pattern:**
```
[Lightning Rod]  [Trident]  [Lightning Rod]
    [Trident]   [Diamond]     [Trident]
[Lightning Rod]  [Trident]  [Lightning Rod]
```

**Materials Required:**
- 4x Lightning Rod
- 4x Trident
- 1x Diamond

**Material Sources:**
- **Lightning Rod:** Craft with 3 copper ingots (12 copper total)
- **Trident:** Rare drop from Drowned
  - Drop rate: 6.25% base, 11.5% with Looting III
  - Only from Drowned holding tridents
- **Diamond:** Mine at Y-level -59 to -64

**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Hard (rare trident drops)

**Farming Tips:**
- Farm drowned in ocean/river biomes
- Use Looting III sword
- Drowned with tridents have higher drop rates
- Expect to kill 10-20 drowned per trident

**Recipe Pattern:**
```
[Dragon Egg] [Dragon Head] [Dragon Egg]
[Dragon Head]  [Diamond]   [Dragon Head]
[Dragon Egg] [Dragon Head] [Dragon Egg]
```

**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ EXTREME (limited dragon eggs!)

- **This is the RAREST Fragment in the game**
- Dragon Eggs are extremely limited (1 per world)
- Requires multiple End city explorations for dragon heads
- Most powerful Fragment with devastating abilities

## Special Item Recipes

### 🔄 Fragment Changer
**Purpose:** Allows switching between Fragments you own

**Recipe Pattern:**
```
[Ender Pearl] [Nether Star] [Ender Pearl]
[Nether Star] [Diamond Block] [Nether Star]
[Ender Pearl] [Nether Star] [Ender Pearl]
```

**Materials Required:**
- 4x Ender Pearl
- 4x Nether Star (requires 4 Wither boss kills!)
- 1x Diamond Block (9 diamonds)

**How to Use:**
1. Craft the Fragment Changer (Ender Eye item)
2. Place it on the ground
3. Wait 5 minutes (stay within 5 blocks)
4. Ritual completes automatically
5. Use `/fragment activate <type>` to switch Fragments

**Cooldown:** 1 hour between Fragment switches

**Difficulty:** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ Very Hard (requires multiple Wither kills)

## Tips & Strategies

### 🎯 Recommended Progression Order

1. **Beginner (Rank 2-3):**
   - Start with **Earth** (easiest materials)
   - Then **Water** or **Fire** (accessible with exploration)
   - **Air** is also good for mobility

2. **Intermediate (Rank 4-5):**
   - **Void** (if you've reached The End)
   - **Dark** or **Light** (requires boss farming)
   - **Mob** (very challenging, save for later)

3. **Advanced (Rank 6-8):**
   - **Storm** (requires trident farming)
   - **Dragon** (ultimate endgame goal)

### 💡 Material Farming Tips

**Wither Skeleton Skulls (Dark Fragment):**
- Use Looting III sword (essential!)
- Build a farm in Nether fortress
- Expect 20-40 kills per skull
- Bring fire resistance potions

**Tridents (Storm Fragment):**
- Farm drowned in ocean biomes
- Use Looting III sword
- Target drowned holding tridents
- Expect 10-20 kills per trident

**Beacons (Light Fragment):**
- Need to defeat Wither boss 4 times
- Requires 12 wither skeleton skulls total
- Build a safe Wither fighting arena
- Bring regeneration and strength potions

**Dragon Eggs (Dragon Fragment):**
- ⚠️ **ONLY 1 EGG SPAWNS NATURALLY**
- Respawning Ender Dragon does NOT drop eggs
- This makes Dragon Fragment extremely rare
- Plan carefully before crafting!

**Spawners (Mob Fragment):**
- Requires Silk Touch pickaxe
- Find in dungeons, mineshafts, strongholds
- Mark locations on map
- Bring Ender Chest to store safely

### ⚙️ Crafting Efficiency

**Mana Flask Mass Production:**
- Lapis is common at Y-level 0 to -64
- Glass bottles are cheap (3 glass each)
- Craft in bulk for long adventures
- Keep 10-20 in inventory

**Fragment Changer Planning:**
- Very expensive (4 Wither kills!)
- Only craft when you own multiple Fragments
- 1-hour cooldown between switches
- Plan your Fragment usage strategically

### ⚠️ Important Warnings

**Ritual Requirements:**
- Duration: 10-15 minutes per Fragment
- Must stay within 5 blocks
- Cannot have multiple rituals active
- Leaving area cancels the ritual

**Dragon Fragment Caution:**
- Dragon Eggs are LIMITED (1 per world)
- Cannot be duplicated or respawned
- Think carefully before crafting
- Most powerful but rarest Fragment

**First Join Bonus:**
- New players receive a random Fragment automatically
- This helps you start without crafting
- You can still craft additional Fragments

## Troubleshooting

### Recipe Not Working?
- ✓ Using 3x3 crafting table (not 2x2 inventory grid)
- ✓ Exact materials (no substitutions)
- ✓ Correct pattern placement
- ✓ Materials have correct names/types

### Ritual Not Starting?
- ✓ Place item on ground (don't right-click)
- ✓ No other rituals active
- ✓ Not on cooldown
- ✓ Correct item (check display name)

### Can't Find Materials?
- Check "Material Sources" for each recipe
- Some require specific dimensions (Nether, End)
- Rare drops require Looting III
- Be patient with rare materials
