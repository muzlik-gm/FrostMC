# Fragment Crafting Recipes

All recipes create a **Fragment Creation** item that must be placed to start a ritual.

## Basic Fragments (Standard 3x3 Pattern: OIO/ICI/OIO)

### Fire Fragment
```
Netherrack | Fire Charge | Netherrack
Fire Charge | Diamond | Fire Charge
Netherrack | Fire Charge | Netherrack
```
