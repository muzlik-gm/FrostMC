## How Fragment Crafting Works

### Step-by-Step Process

1. **Gather Materials** - Collect the required items for your desired Fragment
2. **Open Crafting Table** - Use a 3x3 crafting table
3. **Place Items** - Follow the recipe pattern exactly
4. **Craft the Item** - You'll receive a Fragment Creation item (Nether Star)
5. **Place the Item** - Place the Nether Star on the ground to start the ritual
6. **Complete Ritual** - Stay within 5 blocks for 10-15 minutes
7. **Receive Fragment** - The Fragment will be granted to you automatically

### Recipe Pattern Legend
```
[O] = Outer Material
[I] = Inner Material  
[C] = Center Material (Always Diamond)

Pattern:
[O] [I] [O]
[I] [C] [I]
[O] [I] [O]
```

## Special Items

### 🔄 Fragment Changer
**Purpose:** Allows you to switch between Fragments you own

**Recipe:**
```
[Ender Pearl] [Nether Star] [Ender Pearl]
[Nether Star] [Diamond Block] [Nether Star]
[Ender Pearl] [Nether Star] [Ender Pearl]
```

**Materials Needed:**
- 4x Ender Pearl
- 4x Nether Star
- 1x Diamond Block (9 diamonds)

**How to Use:**
1. Craft the Fragment Changer
2. Place it on the ground
3. Wait 5 minutes (stay within 5 blocks)
4. Use `/fragment activate <type>` to switch
5. Cooldown: 1 hour between switches

**⚠️ Note:** Requires defeating the Wither boss multiple times for nether stars

## Tips & Tricks

### 💡 Farming Tips

**For Wither Skeleton Skulls (Dark Fragment):**
- Use Looting III sword
- Build a farm in Nether fortress
- Expect to kill 20-40 wither skeletons per skull

**For Tridents (Storm Fragment):**
- Farm drowned in ocean biomes
- Use Looting III sword
- Drowned with tridents have higher drop rates

**For Dragon Eggs (Dragon Fragment):**
- Only 1 egg spawns naturally per world
- Respawning Ender Dragon does NOT drop more eggs
- This makes Dragon Fragment the rarest!

**For Beacons (Light Fragment):**
- Requires defeating Wither boss
- Need 3 wither skeleton skulls + 4 soul sand
- Farm wither skeletons in Nether fortress

### 🎯 Recommended Crafting Order

1. **Start:** Earth or Water (easiest materials)
2. **Early Game:** Fire or Air (accessible materials)
3. **Mid Game:** Storm or Void (requires exploration)
4. **Late Game:** Dark or Light (requires boss fights)
5. **End Game:** Mob (requires advanced items)
6. **Ultimate:** Dragon (requires multiple End trips)

### ⚠️ Important Notes

- **Ritual Duration:** 10-15 minutes per Fragment
- **Stay Close:** Must remain within 5 blocks during ritual
- **One at a Time:** Can only have one ritual active
- **Switching:** Requires Fragment Changer + 1 hour cooldown
- **First Join:** You get a random Fragment automatically
- **Progression:** Start with easier Fragments, work up to harder ones

## Troubleshooting

**Recipe not working?**
- Make sure you're using a 3x3 crafting table (not 2x2)
- Check you have the exact materials
- Verify the pattern matches exactly

**Ritual not starting?**
- Place the item (don't right-click)
- Make sure you don't have another ritual active
- Check you're not on cooldown

**Can't find materials?**
- Check the "Where to Find" section for each Fragment
- Some materials require specific dimensions (Nether, End)
- Some materials are rare drops - be patient!
